Frequently asked questions

How do I track my shipment?

Open the Track tab, enter your tracking ID and select Track shipment. You will see the current status, the route progress and a full timeline of every update so far.

Where do I find my tracking ID?

Your tracking ID is shown as soon as you create a consignment on the Ship tab. It starts with OTL followed by 10 digits. Copy it and share it with your receiver so they can follow the parcel too.

How long will my delivery take?

Express delivers in about 2 days, Standard in about 5 days and Economy in about 8 days from the time of booking. The estimated delivery date is shown on your tracking page.

What do the tracking statuses mean?

A shipment moves from Shipment created and Picked up, through the origin facility, transit and the destination hub, then customs clearance and Out for delivery, and ends at Delivered. The status marked as current is where your parcel is now.

Why does my tracking ID say it can’t be found?

Check that the ID has no spaces and is typed in full, for example OTL followed by 10 digits. IDs are saved on the device where the shipment was created, so try tracking from that same device or browser.
